# Connect the radio

## Connect the radio

1. Switch the radio on and connect it to the computer with the USB cable.
2. The radio asks for the USB mode. Choose **Serial** (not drive/storage).
3. In Lua-Studio, choose the radio under **Connection** at the top. Ethos radios are marked with **●** and are selected automatically. If it says “No radio found”: check cable and mode, then click **Search again**.
4. Click **Connect**. The status line shows “Please allow remote control on the radio”.
5. **Confirm** the remote control prompt on the radio. Lua-Studio waits up to 2 minutes.
6. The status line shows **Connected**, the radio and the Ethos version. The display appears shortly after.

 Every new connection has to be allowed on the radio again, also after disconnecting and reconnecting.

## Operate

| On the computer | On the radio |
|---|---|
| Click into the picture | tap |
| Right-click, or hold the mouse button | long tap |
| Drag with the mouse button held | swipe |
| Mouse wheel (pointer over the picture), arrow keys ↑ ↓ (or ← →) | rotary encoder |
| Enter · Esc or Backspace · Tab | ENTER · RTN · PAGE |
| F1 · F2 · F3 | SYS · MDL · DISP |
| Shift + key | long key press |

On the right there are buttons for all keys. With **Long press** ticked, the next button is pressed long. Text for the radio's on-screen keyboard (e.g. a model name) goes into **Text for the on-screen keyboard**, then click **Send**. A text field must be open on the radio first.

The shortcuts work anywhere in the window, except while you type into an input field or a selection list.

### Size, screenshot, log

- **Size**: Fit (fills the window), 100 %, 150 % or 200 %.
- **Screenshot** saves the current display as PNG (suggested folder: Pictures).
- **Lua log of the radio** shows what Lua scripts on the radio print and error messages (red). **Clear** empties it.
- **DE / EN** at the top right switches the language.
